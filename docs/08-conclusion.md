# 8. Conclusion

This project connects discretionary and quant trading in a way that can be measured. Model A turns ten years of prices and headlines into regime and mood labels. Model B learns how prices behaved in each market state. A trader can then replace Model A's labels with their own view and see how the prediction changes.

The main contribution is the break-even accuracy: a clear answer to how often a trader's judgement must be right before it beats the numbers alone. Along the way, the experiment ladder shows what raw quant facts buy, whether today's news is already priced in, and what perfect judgement could be worth.

Every part uses classical machine learning and covers the course requirements. The market, horizon and news source are still to be decided, and the model choices in Chapter 6 need more work before we commit to them.
